Spatia

AI-powered spatial intelligence for designing and visualizing your home.

Status: React Native prototype — native ARKit/RealityKit spatial layer planned.

Spatia is a mobile interior-design concept that aims to combine AI, computer vision, spatial computing, and 3D visualization to help users understand and redesign their homes.

✨ Concept

Users capture their room with their phone, and Spatia will eventually analyze the space, understand its layout, and help generate personalized interior designs.

🎯 Vision

Spatia aims to become an intelligent bridge between the physical home and digital design, allowing users to scan, redesign, visualize, and interact with their spaces through AI and spatial computing.
