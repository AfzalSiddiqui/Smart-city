Spatia

AI-powered spatial intelligence for designing and visualizing your home.

Spatia is a mobile AI application that uses computer vision, spatial computing, generative AI, AR, and 3D rendering to transform real-world rooms into interactive digital spaces.

✨ Key Features
📷 Room Scanning — Capture and understand rooms using the camera.
🧠 AI Spatial Analysis — Detect walls, floors, furniture, windows, and room dimensions.
🎨 AI Interior Design — Generate personalized designs based on style, color, and preferences.
🧊 3D Visualization — Explore redesigned rooms with interactive 3D models.
📱 AR Furniture — Place and visualize furniture directly in the real environment.
🤖 AI Designer — Ask an AI assistant to modify layouts and recommend improvements.
🎥 Visual Sharing — Capture and share redesigned spaces.
🛠️ Technology

Swift · SwiftUI · Vision · Core ML · ARKit · RealityKit · Metal · AVFoundation · Generative AI

🎯 Goal

Build an intelligent bridge between the physical home and digital design, allowing users to scan, redesign, visualize, and interact with their spaces using AI and spatial computing.
